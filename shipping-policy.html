1. Delivery Area

We currently deliver to all cities and towns across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. This includes Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Warangal, Rajahmundry, Guntur, Kakinada, Nellore, and surrounding areas. We do not ship outside these two states at this time.

2. Dispatch Timelines

Ready-to-wear ensembles are typically dispatched within 1 to 3 business days. Made-to-measure bridal couture timelines are specified on the individual product page during checkout.

3. Insured Packaging

To protect delicate silk weaves, zari borders, and gemstone embroideries, orders are dispatched in custom climate-resistant garment bags and reinforced presentation boxes.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges, if any, are displayed during checkout on our e-commerce store. Free delivery may be available on orders above a certain value.
