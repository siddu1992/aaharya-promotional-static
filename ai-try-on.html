Our Biometric & Photo Privacy Safeguards

When you utilize the AI Virtual Try-On feature on our e-commerce platform:

  • Images are processed in temporary encrypted memory strictly to render garment drape.
  • We do not harvest biometric databases or sell personal imagery to third parties.
  • Uploaded photos are not used to train public AI models without explicit consent.
  • You retain full rights to delete your uploaded fitting photos at any time.
