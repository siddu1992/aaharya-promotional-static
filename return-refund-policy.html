Return & Refund Policy

Easy returns and exchanges for customers across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Self-Service Portal

Initiate a Return or Exchange

Purchases made on our online store can be managed, returned, or exchanged via your customer order dashboard.

Open Returns Portal

1. Who Can Return

Our return and exchange policy applies to customers who placed orders and received delivery within Andhra Pradesh or Telangana.

2. Return Window & Condition

Ready-to-wear items can be returned within the standard return window shown on your order page. Items must have original tags and security ribbons intact and must be unused and unwashed.

3. Bespoke Bridal Exclusions

Custom-made bridal lehengas and tailored made-to-measure outfits are crafted specifically to your personal measurements and cannot be returned. Complimentary fit alterations are provided by our team upon request.

4. How to Initiate a Return

Log in to your account on our e-commerce store and raise a return request from your orders page. Our team will arrange a pickup from your address in AP or Telangana within 2 business days.
