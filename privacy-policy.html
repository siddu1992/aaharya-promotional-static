01.Introduction & Scope

Anvayaa Texch Hub. (“Anvayaa,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is an Indian fashion house registered under the Companies Act, 2013, based at 65-7-6, Sri Ram Krishnanagar, Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh, India.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website at anvayaafashion.com, our AI Virtual Try-On feature, our newsletter system, our contact and inquiry forms, and any other digital services operated by Anvayaa. It does not cover the data practices of our linked e-commerce platform, which has its own privacy documentation.

We comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

03.AI Virtual Try-On Photo Safeguards

Special Biometric Data Clause

Photographs uploaded by users for AI Virtual Try-On are classified as sensitive personal data and are subject to heightened protections under applicable data protection law.

Encrypted Processing

Photos are processed in transient, end-to-end encrypted server memory. They are never written to persistent disk storage.

No Third-Party Sale

We never sell, rent, or commercially license your photographs to any third party, advertiser, or data broker.

No Facial Recognition

We do not build biometric identification databases, facial fingerprints, or use your image for identity verification purposes.

Instant Deletion

Try-On images are automatically purged from our processing servers within 60 minutes of your session ending.

AI Training Consent: Uploaded photographs are not used to train public machine learning foundation models without your explicit, affirmative consent. Users may opt-in to contribute anonymized silhouette data to improve our fitting algorithm through a separate, clearly-labeled consent flow.

04.How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Responding to Inquiries: To process and respond to concierge styling inquiries, bespoke bridal appointment requests, and general customer service communications.
  • VIP Communications: To send seasonal lookbook launches, exclusive preview invitations, limited collection announcements, and promotional campaign updates to subscribed members.
  • AI Try-On Service Delivery: To process uploaded photographs and generate garment drape simulations, returning visual output to the requesting user only.
  • Website Analytics & Improvement: To understand user behavior, improve page layouts, optimize collection showcase performance, and identify technical issues.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To monitor for suspicious access patterns, prevent unauthorized scraping of editorial content, and ensure platform integrity.
  • Legal Compliance: To meet obligations under applicable Indian data protection and intellectual property laws, including the IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023.

06.Cookies & Web Analytics

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve our website. The types of cookies we deploy are:

Required for core website functionality such as session state, navigation preferences, and security tokens. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

07.Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with carefully vetted third-party service providers solely to operate our website and services:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Provider: For secure web hosting and server operation (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud), bound by data processing agreements.
  • Email Delivery Service: To send transactional and VIP newsletter emails on our behalf. Receives only your email address and name.
  • Website Analytics Provider: To collect aggregated usage statistics. Data is pseudonymized and does not identify you individually.
  • AI Processing Engine: Our AI try-on feature may use a specialist AI inference provider. Photographs are transmitted via encrypted channels and processed under strict data minimization agreements.
  • Legal & Compliance Authorities: We may disclose your information when required by law, court order, or governmental regulatory authority.

All third-party providers are contractually bound to process your data only for specified purposes and in compliance with applicable data protection law.

08.Data Storage

Most data processing and storage occurs within India. Images uploaded and generated for our AI Virtual Try-On feature are stored on Cloudflare R2 — a secure, globally distributed object storage service operated by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare R2 provides encrypted storage and fast delivery of generated try-on imagery.

Cloudflare R2 storage may be located in data centres across India and internationally. All transfers are protected by strict security safeguards, and images are automatically deleted by our scheduled background service based on the system retention schedule (configurable by authorized administration). By using the AI Virtual Try-On feature, you consent to this storage and lifecycle management arrangement.

11.Security Measures

We implement industry-leading technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data:

  • TLS/SSL Encryption: All data transmission between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 or higher.
  • Access Controls: Personal data is accessible only to authorized personnel with a demonstrable business need, enforced through role-based access controls (RBAC).
  • Data Pseudonymization: Where possible, analytics and processing data is pseudonymized to reduce risk in the event of an unauthorized access incident.
  • Security Audits: Our infrastructure undergoes periodic security assessments and vulnerability scans by qualified security professionals.
  • Incident Response: We maintain a documented data breach response procedure. In the event of a breach affecting your rights, we will notify you and relevant supervisory authorities within 72 hours as required by law.

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no Internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords for any accounts and to report any suspected security vulnerabilities to security@anvayaafashion.com.

12.Children's Privacy

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a minor, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our records. If you believe a child under 18 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at privacy@anvayaafashion.com.
